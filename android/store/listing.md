# Play Store listing (copy and paste)

**App name** (30 max): `Nifti: Orders, Stock & Books`

**Short description** (80 max):
`Orders, stock, money and your Viber chats in one app for PH businesses.`

**Full description** (4000 max):

Nifti runs the day-to-day of a Philippine business: what customers ordered, what to buy from suppliers, who owes you, what's in stock and where the money went. On your phone and on any computer, with the same account.

WHAT NEEDS YOU TODAY
• One Home screen with today's orders, cash to count, money owed and stock running low
• Red counts that stay until the thing is actually done

ORDERS, START TO PAID
• Customer orders and supplier orders side by side
• Photos of invoices, receipts and delivery proof saved with each order
• Know what's coming in and what's due out this week

YOUR VIBER CHATS, INSIDE NIFTI
• Customer and supplier Viber messages land next to their orders
• Photos and files from Viber come in by themselves
• Your team replies from Nifti; the reply goes out from your own Viber
• Personal chats (family, friends) stay out of Nifti

GETTING PAID
• Who owes you, how much and since when
• Reminders by Viber, SMS or email in one tap

STOCK
• Know what's running low before it runs out
• Count stock by phone and see what went missing

MONEY AND BOOKS
• Sales, expenses, profit for the month
• Bills coming up, with a photo of each one
• Export everything to Excel any time

YOUR TEAM
• Tasks, team chat and activity
• Each person only sees what their role allows

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your business data is only visible to your own team
• Nifti only reads Viber, only after you say yes, and never sells or shares your data

Works on Android 8 and newer. Also on iPhone (add to home screen) and any computer at siggiebahamas.github.io/Nifti.
