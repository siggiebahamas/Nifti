Get Nifti on your phone

Same account everywhere. Pick your phone below.

AndroidApp

The full app: your Nifti icon, instant alerts, and your Viber chats inside Nifti.

Download the app
  1. When it finishes, tap Open.
  2. If the phone asks, tap Settings, allow your browser to install apps, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then Open, and sign in.
If Play Protect says it doesn't know this app, tap More details, then Install anyway. That shows for any app not yet on the Play Store.

iPhone

Nifti goes on your home screen with its own icon and alerts. Nothing to download.

Open Nifti in Safari
  1. In Safari, tap Share at the bottom.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Nifti from the new icon and allow alerts.
It has to be Safari. Chrome on iPhone can't add it with alerts.
