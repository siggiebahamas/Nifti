C. The order card: less crowded

Today the order number, status pill and item share one line with empty space after it, the reason wraps under them, and the green button is squeezed into the bottom-right corner. Three ways to give each thing its own place.

1. Reason first, button full width

MY PICK
They say they paid. Check your bank or GCash.
Order 0810
300 × Steel Rebar 12mm
₱17,504 owes you

What needs doing is the first thing you read. The order and amount sit on one row under it. The button runs the full width, so it is never squeezed. The "Billed" pill goes away: the sentence already says where the order is.

2. Two columns: facts left, action right

Order 0810 Billed
300 × Steel Rebar 12mm
They say they paid.
₱17,504

The right side holds only the amount and the button, lined up. The left holds the order. The long explanation is cut to four words.

3. Status as a header strip

They say they paid · check your bank or GCash
Order 0810
300 × Steel Rebar 12mm
₱17,504

The status becomes a colored strip across the top of the card, so every card in a band starts with the same shape. Order, amount and button follow.
