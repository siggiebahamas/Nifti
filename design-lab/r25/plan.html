No first-day setup: great ways to begin

Today a new shop lands on empty pages. The best first day is one where they type almost nothing, and by lunch Nifti already knows their items, customers and who owes them.

1

Snap your notebook and price list

The owner takes photos of their utang notebook and their price list. The notebook reader fills in items, prices, customers and what each one owes. They check each line with a yes.

About 30 minutes
2

Pick your kind of shop

Hardware, sari-sari, food supply, construction supply. Nifti loads the 50 most common items for that kind of shop with typical prices; they delete what they don't carry and fix prices.

About 10 minutes
3

Send us your file, or we set it up

Upload an Excel or an old POS export and Nifti maps the columns. Or pick done-for-you setup (already in the app as a paid service): your team enters their items and customers within 2 days.

5 minutes for them
4

Invite 3 suppliers and 5 customers

Each invite is one Viber or Messenger message. Suppliers who join bring their price list; customers who join send orders straight in. Every new shop also becomes a reason for their partners to join.

About 5 minutes
My recommended first week (to build once you go live)
  1. Sign-up asks one question: "What kind of shop?" and loads the starter items (way 2), so nothing is empty.
  2. Home shows 3 big buttons: "Snap your notebook" (way 1), "Send us your Excel" (way 3), "Invite a supplier" (way 4). The Get set up checklist stays, with these on top.
  3. Telegram check-ins on day 1, 3 and 7 (the reminders already exist): "Snap one page of your utang notebook today."
  4. First 20 sign-ups get done-for-you setup free. You learn exactly where people get stuck, and they become your first success stories.
