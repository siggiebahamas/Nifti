Buying from suppliers: 5 colors (orange and gray)

12345

What you're looking at

The Orders page with the selling side in blue and five colors for the buying side: three oranges and two grays. In all five, "All good" is the new lighter forest band with a green check on a white circle, and the order bubbles inside it are soft green again, like before Round 24. Both of those are already live in the app.

My pick is 4, cool gray. Nifti uses red, yellow and blue to mean "do it now", "coming up" and "waiting on them". An orange side sits right between red and yellow, so a 65-year-old owner may read a calm supplier column as a warning. Gray says "the other side" without saying "hurry".

1. Tangerine

Bright orange edge and title

Strongest contrast with the blue side. The risk: the orange title is close to the red and yellow urgency bands.

2. Peach

Soft orange, very light page

The gentlest orange. Calm and warm, but on a dim phone screen it can look almost white, so the two sides blur together.

3. Apricot

Mid orange with a warm tint behind

A middle ground: you see right away which side is buying. The tinted background is warmer than the selling side's.

4. Cool gray

MY PICK

Neutral gray edge and dark title. Blue means "money coming to you", gray means "your suppliers". The color bands stay the only colors that mean "act".

5. Graphite

Darker gray, more weight

Like 4, but heavier, so the buying side feels as important as the selling side. A bit more serious.
