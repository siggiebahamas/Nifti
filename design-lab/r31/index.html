Messages: the list on the left, 3 ways

Every option uses the same three colors so you can spot a chat type instantly: Team amber, Customers blue, Suppliers green. Unread counts are red, and unread chats are bold, like Facebook. All three are clickable. The sample shop has 14 customers and 9 suppliers.

A. Three colored tabs My pick

One group at a time, so the list never gets long. Each tab shows its red unread count, so you know where to look without opening it. "Unread" shows only what needs you.

Best for: shops with many customers. You only ever scroll one group.
Trade-off: you can't see all three groups at once (red counts on the tabs cover that).

B. Colored sections you can fold

Everything on one list, as today, but each group has a bold colored header that stays on screen while you scroll. Only the 4 most recent chats show; "See all" opens the rest. Fold away a group you don't need.

Best for: owners who like seeing everything at once.
Trade-off: it still grows longer as you add people, just more slowly.

C. Icon rail, like Slack or Viber desktop

A narrow strip of big colored buttons on the far left (All, Team, Customers, Suppliers), each with a red count. The list beside it shows the group you picked. It's the fastest to switch with one tap, and works well on a phone too.

Best for: people who jump between groups all day.
Trade-off: takes a little width on small laptops.

Team: a command center, 3 ways

The Team page becomes the place where the owner runs the day: who is doing what, what's late, and one-tap assigning. Everything below works: drag cards, tick tasks done, assign jobs.

A. Mission board My pick

Three columns: To do, Doing, Done. Drag a card to move it. The day's score sits on top (done, late, still open), and each person has a progress bar for the day. Ticking a task gives a small win. It's simple enough for staff to use on a phone.

B. Squad cards + live feed

Each person is a card: where they are (in the shop, on delivery, off), what they're doing now, and their week's progress ring. Tap a card to give them a job or nudge them. A live feed on the right shows everything happening in the shop, like a game's activity log.

C. Day map + weekly quests

A timeline of today with one row per person (like a game schedule). Colored blocks are jobs; the red line is now. Tap a block to finish it. Below are the week's team goals with progress bars, plus a friendly leaderboard.

Help menu: 3 ways

In all three, "Help" is a plain row at the bottom of the left menu with a 1 red count beside it when the Nifti team answers. That's separate from the bell on top, so replies never get lost. Click "Help" in each mockup.

A. Chat drawer, like Messenger My pick

Help slides in from the right as a chat with the Nifti team: real faces, "usually answers in an hour", and new replies marked in red. Quick question chips sit above the message box.

B. Help center with search

A big window with a search bar ("How do I…"), short guides as cards, a Messages tab for chatting with the team, and "My questions" showing which ones got an answer (red when unread).

C. Bottom sheet that knows your page

Help rises from the bottom and starts with steps for the page you're on (e.g. Expenses: how to snap a receipt). Below that are three big buttons: Message us, Watch a 1-minute guide, and Call/Viber. Small "?" buttons on pages open the same sheet.
