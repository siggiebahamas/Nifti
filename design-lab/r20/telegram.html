Nifti and Telegram: 10 ways they work together

Telegram lets a business app send messages with buttons, read photos you send it, and answer questions, for free. Here's what that could do for a Nifti owner, their staff and their customers, each with a sample chat. The setup guide and practice chats are now inside Nifti (Alerts on your phone). Real messages start once Nifti's Telegram account is connected.

1Connect onceOpen Nifti, tap "Connect Telegram", and scan the code with your phone. Each staff member does the same on their own phone.
2Nifti gets its own Telegram accountMessages come from "Nifti · Dela Cruz Hardware", like a helper in your chat list. Tap a button and Nifti does it.
3Nothing extra to pay per messageTelegram doesn't charge for these messages. Customers who don't use Telegram keep getting the usual Nifti link.
My top 3 to build first: 1 · Morning brief, 2 · Approve with one tap, 4 · Payment proof matched for you. They save the owner the most time every single day and are the simplest to build.

How someone who has never used Telegram learns it

Nobody reads a manual. The learning happens inside Nifti and inside the chat itself.

1A setup guide inside Nifti"Alerts on your phone" walks through 4 steps with big numbers: get Telegram, tap Open, tap START, Nifti says hello. It's in the left menu and your account menu now.
2Practice before it's realAll 10 alerts can be tried inside Nifti first. Tap Approve, see what happens. Nothing is sent.
3One group, a folder for each jobNifti makes your team group with folders (Telegram calls them topics): Daily brief, Approvals, Payments, Cash count, Running low, Orders to pack, Deliveries, Ask Nifti. Each person only joins the folders their role needs.
4Help inside every messageEvery alert has a "?" button that explains it in Taglish or English. For the first week Nifti sends one short tip a day. Typing "help" reaches a person.

How it gets built, in plain words

1One Nifti account on TelegramMade once by Nifti for all its users. Your business doesn't make or manage anything.
2A small program on Nifti's serverIt sends the messages and listens for button taps. A tap does exactly what the same button does in Nifti, with the same roles and records.
3People link their phone onceThe 4-step START links each person's Telegram to their Nifti login, so Nifti knows who is who and what they're allowed to see.
4TimersThe morning brief and closing-time cash count run on a timer you set.
5Reading photos and questionsPayment screenshots and "Magkano utang ni Bayview?" are read by the same AI that turns Viber messages into orders today.
6Customers stay separateCustomers chat one-on-one with Nifti for orders, reminders and price news. They never see your team group.
What I need from you to switch it on (about 2 minutes):
  1. Install Telegram on your phone and sign up with your number.
  2. Open the chat called BotFather, type /newbot, and name it "Nifti". It gives you a long code.
  3. Put that code into Nifti's server settings (I'll show you exactly where). Don't paste it in a chat.
Then I connect the server and the 10 alerts start working for real.
