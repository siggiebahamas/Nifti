When a key person leaves and the notebooks stay behind

Three steps: read the old paper into Nifti, hand over everything the person was holding before they go, and keep what they knew on each customer's page so it never walks out the door again. Everything on this page is a prototype.

The notebook is half in their headWho owes what, which customer gets which price, who pays late. Written in shorthand only they can read.
Nobody knows what's still openOrders they promised, money they were collecting, chats they were answering. It all goes quiet the day they leave.
The history is lostYears of sales and prices sit in boxes of paper nobody will ever open again.

Snap the notebook. Nifti types it in for you.

Take a photo of each page of the utang list, the price notebook or the delivery book. Nifti reads the handwriting and turns it into rows. You check each one and tap OK.
