Your day, hour by hour: 5 more ways

12345

What each one fixes

  • Times you can read: every time slot is a solid block or bold dark text, never see-through on dark green.
  • Summary boxes you can read: "One place" and "Fewer calls" sit on solid white or solid dark boxes with an icon.
  • "Before" you can read: dark text on a plain light box. No faint text, no crossing out.
  • Same size boxes: each hour's "before" and "with Nifti" share one row, so they're always the same height.

1. Light page, dark time blocks

MY PICK

The easiest to read. The section turns light, so the time blocks (solid slate blue) and the two summary boxes stand out. Before is a white card; with Nifti is soft green. Feels like reading a day planner.

2. Keep the dark green, make it solid

Same look as today, fixed

Keeps your current dark green. Times become solid yellow blocks that match the "What changes" tag; before is solid light gray, after is white with a green edge. Summary boxes are solid white.

3. A timeline down the page

Like a clock running through the day

A line with a dot for each hour. Each hour is one card split in half, so before and after are always the same size. The summary boxes are dark green, the strongest thing on the page.

4. Night to day

Deep navy, before dark, after bright

The change you feel: each "before" is a dark box, each "with Nifti" is bright white, with a green arrow between. Times are outlined in white. Most dramatic of the five.

5. One card per hour, side by side

Five cards, like a week planner

Each hour is its own card: a dark time header, "before" on top, "with Nifti" below. Five cards across on a computer; one under the other on a phone. Boxes are the same size by design.
