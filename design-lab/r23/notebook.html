A. One big button: snap, check, done

MY PICK

The fewest decisions. One button opens the camera. Nifti works out the kind of page by itself and says it plainly ("Looks like your sales"); one tap changes it if it's wrong. Lines it isn't sure about are yellow. Then it adds everything and takes you to the right page. Your past pages are listed so you can see what's already in.

B. Pick the page first, then one question at a time

Six big tiles

For people who like to be in control: tap what the page is, point the camera inside the yellow frame, then answer one short question per unsure line (most answers are "Yes"). Slower, but nothing surprises anyone.

C. Chat with Nifti, like Viber

Feels like texting

Everyone already knows how to send a photo in a chat. Nifti answers like a person: what it found, what it isn't sure about, and two buttons. It can also offer the next step, like sending reminders to the customers it just added.
