Sales vs Orders: why both, and my recommendation

OrdersSales
Who it's forCustomers who order ahead: contractors, other shopsWalk-in buyers at the counter
What happensConfirm, prepare, deliver, bill, get paid laterPay now (or utang) and take the items home
MoneyInvoices, due dates, remindersThe cash drawer, counted every night by person

My recommendation: keep both, but rename Sales to "Counter & cash" and put the cash drawer and the count-by-person section at the top, since that's why owners open it. The walk-in sales list goes underneath. For a shop that only sells by order (a distributor), the page is hidden by the shop-type setting above.
