Is Nifti ready to launch? A full run-through

ScorecardMust fixFix soonEvery pageWordsPrototypePlan

The short answer

Ready for a hands-on pilot with 5 to 10 shops that you set up yourself. Not ready yet for strangers to sign up alone, or to charge money.

The core loop works and feels calm: order, deliver, bill, collect, and the books fill in by themselves. What holds it back is not the idea or the look. It's four things: some features look real but aren't yet, the menu gives a 65-year-old too many choices, Filipino mode is half English, and a few trust basics (security pass, data export, privacy notice, price) aren't finished.

How I checked: I opened all 22 pages plus the notebook reader, alerts, landing, pricing, privacy and terms, on a computer (1440 wide) and a phone (390 wide). I counted words, buttons and tiny text on each page, switched to Filipino, measured load size, and ran the database's security checker.

Scorecard

Must fix before launch

In the order I'd do them.

1Anything that only pretends must be finished or hidden

Telegram alerts aren't connected yet, the notebook reader reads a sample page, post reminders don't send, team permission changes and supplier credit slips save only on one phone, store results are example numbers, and "Export my data" is a stub. If a new owner taps one and nothing real happens, they stop trusting the real parts too.

For launch, show each as "Coming soon" with a "Tell me when it's ready" button, or make it real. Keep them fully working in the demo.

2A simple menu for first-time users

23 menu lines plus the same pages again as pills at the top of each page. Your users will freeze at "Payroll, Prices, To check, Books, Checks, Tax books, BIR 2307s…".

Start everyone on a 7-line menu: Home, Orders, Messages, Stock, Money, Selling, Notebook reader. One "Show all tools" link opens the rest. On computers, drop the duplicate pill row (keep it on phones, where there's no side menu).

3Finish Filipino, or hide the switch

In FIL mode the Orders page says "MGA ORDER · 6 pages here · Customers on the left, suppliers on the right · NEEDS YOU NOW · PAPARATING". The whole store, returns and books pages stay English.

Translate rounds 20 to 24 text (about 600 lines), using the same Taglish tone as the store posts. Until then, hide the FIL button.

4Security pass on the database

The security checker lists 45 database functions anyone can call without signing in, and 187 any signed-in user can call. Most check permission inside (32 of the 33 admin ones do). One doesn't: admin_funnel_data lets any signed-in user read Nifti-wide counts (number of businesses, orders this week). I tried to lock it with a one-line change, but database changes need your OK.

Approve the one-line fix. Then check each of the 45 public functions: every one should require a secret link code or only return public store info. Also move the pg_net extension out of the public schema.

5A real first day: "Bring your shop in"

There's an import for stock, but nothing that brings in customers, suppliers and what each one owes in one sitting. The first hour decides whether a shop stays.

One guided screen: 1) items and prices (Excel, or a photo of your price list), 2) customers and their utang (the notebook reader), 3) suppliers and what you owe, 4) cash in the drawer today. Then Home fills up immediately.

6Decide the price and how people pay

The Pricing page still shows Free vs Pro (₱990, free in the pilot), and "PRO · free in pilot" tags still appear inside the app, though you asked in Round 20 to drop free vs pro from the landing page.

Pick one launch offer, say it in one line, remove the PRO tags, and show how to pay (GCash or bank) with a receipt.

7Careful with "for the BIR"

Tax books and BIR 2307s are labeled as BIR books. Using software as your official books of account normally needs BIR registration first.

Until that's in place, call them "Summaries for your bookkeeper" and say so on the page.

8Update the privacy notice and terms

Both are dated September 22, before notebook photos, Telegram alerts, store posts and staff tasks existed.

Add what each new feature stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

9Test the first open on a cheap phone with mobile data

1.4 MB on first download is fine on Wi-Fi, slow on prepaid data outside the city.

Try it on a ₱5,000-class Android on mobile data. Add a simple "Opening Nifti…" screen. Later, load Books, Tax and Store only when opened.

Fix in the first weeks

APages too long on a phone

Orders 4,945 px tall (about 6 screens), Prices 5,191, Money overview 4,630, Expenses 4,386, Counter 4,198.

Show the top 3 of each list with "See all"; on Orders show one side at a time on a phone (Selling | Buying switch with counts).

BToo many buttons on one page

Counter 53, Expenses 49, Orders 45, Store 38, Home 29. Every sale line has Receipt and Remove; every expense has Edit and Remove.

One main button per page. Line actions appear only after tapping a line.

CText that's too small

Labels under 13 px: Orders band names (12.5 px), Money chart labels (12 px), Tax books headers (12.5 px), Stock status tags on phone (12.5 px), Counter cash summary on phone (12.9 px).

Make 14 px the smallest anywhere.

DMenu names don't match page titles

Getting paid opens "Money owed", Checks opens "Check payments", Deliveries opens "Delivery routes", Find customers opens "Find customers & suppliers", Books opens "Books & reports".

One name each, the same everywhere.

ESmall gray sub-rows are easy to miss

Getting paid has the same small "In Getting paid: Who owes you · Checks · Cash & bank" row that Books had.

Same fix as Books: remove it, since Checks already has its own menu line, or turn it into big tabs.

FTitle Case leftovers

"Start Stocktake", "Draft Reorder", "Getting Low", "All Warehouses", "Manage warehouses" for a one-store shop.

"Count all stock", "Reorder", "Running low", "All storage places"; hide storage places until a second one is added.

GThe demo is stuck on August 26

The demo says "Today is Wed, Aug 26" while new things (like saved notebook pages) say today's real date.

Shift the demo's dates so its "today" is the real today.

HThe menu's bottom box covers a heading on laptops

"Telegram alerts" and "Who sees what" sit over the "Selling & delivery" heading on shorter screens.

Move "Who sees what" into Settings, and keep only the alerts link at the bottom.

IA real person to ask

"What's this page?" helps, but nervous users want a human.

A "Need help? Message us" line in the menu that opens your own Viber or phone number.

What's genuinely strong (keep it)

  • Both sides in one place. Selling to customers and buying from suppliers, side by side, with the same colors everywhere.
  • Every card says the next step. "Send the bill", "Count it", "Pay", "Answer". No one has to figure out what to do.
  • Money that adds up. One profit number on every page, books that fill in from orders, credits that take themselves off the next bill.
  • It speaks the shop's language. Utang, vale, sukli, Taglish posts and replies.
  • Calm by design. No crossed-out text, gray zeros, yellow "please check" instead of red, and nothing is added without a yes.

A launch plan that fits

1. Two weeks: close the gapsMust-fix 1 to 4 and 6 to 8. Hide what's not real. Simple menu. Filipino finished or hidden.
2. Four weeks: pilot with 5 shopsYou set each one up in person, using "Bring your shop in". Watch: do they open Nifti 5 days out of 7? Do they record sales and collections without you?
3. Then chargeTurn on the price once 3 of the 5 shops would be upset to lose it. Fix what the pilot showed, then open sign-ups.
