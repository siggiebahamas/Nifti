Round 35: menu counts x5, finding customers x3

Menu counts x5Find customers x3

Red counts in the left menu: 5 ways

All five use red so they stand out, but stay calm and businesslike. Each card shows the same menu with counts on Orders, Stock, Money overview and To check. Option 1 is live in the app now, so you can see it in place; tell me if you want another.

Find customers: 3 ways

Leads come from two places, each clearly labeled. On Nifti businesses chose to be found, so you see what they buy and roughly how much a month. From Google businesses are any shop or builder near any area you pick, with their phone and address (we can't see what they buy). Nothing is locked to one area; type any city or "All Philippines". Every lead has a Message button.

A. Clean lead cards My pick

One card per business: name, monthly spend as the big number, what they buy as plain words (no bubbles), why they're a good fit, and three clear buttons.

B. List on the left, full profile on the right

Scan many leads fast (name, type, monthly spend). Tap one to see everything they buy, their monthly amounts, and the buttons, like an inbox.

C. One lead at a time (swipe deck)

A big, exciting card per business. Tap Skip, Save or Message, and the next one slides in. It feels like a game and is great on a phone.
