What changed from last time

More detail where it helps: who is in charge of each problem, how long it has been open, how much money, and what kind it is. No "next step" line. Each row opens to show what happened and the proof. Pick one and tell me.

A. Table you can open

My pick
Best when there are many problems. Everything fits in one row; tap a row to see what happened and the proof. Assign and status change right in the row.

B. List and details side by side

Best for working through problems one by one: the list stays on the left, the full story and buttons on the right. On a phone the details open as a full screen.

C. Board by status

Best to see at a glance who is stuck. Each column is a status; cards move across as they get fixed.
