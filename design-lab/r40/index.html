The minimal strip, bigger, with words that say what to do Recommended

The rule every pill followsTitle = what it is. Big number = how many or how much. Last line = what to do about it, in plain words. If a pill doesn't lead to an action, it says "nothing to do" so nobody wonders. Tap any pill to see those items.
