Header pills: one title, one number

Same shape as the Money overview boxes: long, calm, and they stretch to fit. The title says what it is, the number sits under it, and there is no third line. Each pill has a colored icon so you can tell them apart before you read.

Old words vs new words

The third line is gone. Anything it said that mattered went into the title or got its own pill.

Handwriting test: 100 lines

I read each line without seeing the answer, then a script compared my reading to the real text. 40 lines are real people's handwriting (scanned English notes from a public research set). 60 are shop-notebook lines I made in English, Tagalog and Taglish using 17 handwriting fonts, with ballpen and pencil looks, fading, blur and paper noise.

Be careful with these numbers. The 60 shop lines use computer handwriting fonts, which are neater than a real tindera's notebook. I did the reading here; the app uses a cheaper model that is a bit weaker. The test that counts is 20–30 photos of real Filipino notebooks from your pilot shops. That's why the new Check every line screen makes a person tick every line before anything is saved.
