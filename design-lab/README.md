# Nifti design lab: font themes

Open `/design-lab/` on the Pages site. The lab loads the real app with demo data in a frame and lets you:

- switch between Current and 5 font pairings instantly,
- jump to any page (22 menu pages plus Edit history),
- view desktop or phone size,
- compare two themes side by side on the same page.

You can also click around inside the frame; it's the working app.

Themes are visual only. They change fonts, sizes, weights and capitals. Every feature, page, number, button and permission stays as it is.

## Stage 1 · Audit: what every theme must preserve

- **Menu (22 pages):**
  - Home, Orders, Messages, Customers & suppliers, Stock.
  - Money: Money overview, Sales, Expenses, Cash count, Who owes you, Profit & prices.
  - Bookkeeping: To check, Books & reports, Bank & deposits, Payroll, Month-end close.
  - Sell more: Online store, Price list, Follow-ups.
  - Control: Owner's watchdog, Team.
  - More tools, and Settings, which includes the Activity log ("Edit history").
- **Home (approved Mist design):**
  - 8 headline tiles.
  - What to do (Act Today, This Week, Fine).
  - Where every order is (6 stages, selling and buying).
  - What runs out (days left or pesos on the shelf, with colour levels).
  - Cash drawer by person: calendar, day tally, who counts, optional protections.
- **Shared components:**
  - Sidebar with badges, top bar (search, language, + Create, notifications, account).
  - Page titles, buyer/supplier tabs, KPI tiles and strips, panels and cards.
  - Tables with sortable headers, list rows, chips and filters, segmented tabs.
  - Status pills, buttons (primary, outline, small, danger, row actions), forms and toggles.
  - Bottom sheets and modals, toasts, bottom navigation on phones.
- **Meaning of colour (kept in every theme):**
  - Red: late, short, loss, danger.
  - Yellow: order soon, pending, needs you (never orange).
  - Green: done, healthy, profit.
  - The theme's accent marks the action to take.

## How it is built (design tokens)

- `themes/neutral.css` (generated): replaces the app's hard-coded beige and brown with tokens.
- `themes/mist.css` (generated): maps every Home colour to tokens.
- `themes/base.css`: the engine. Maps each component to tokens and adds shared polish: hover, press, disabled, focus rings, menus and badges.
- `themes/t01…t10-*.css`: each theme sets its tokens, fonts and signature details.

## Round 5 · Current + 5 font pairings (current)

**Current** is now live in the app: the type style of the Home page (Plus Jakarta Sans, bold sentence-case labels, extra-bold numbers, uppercase table headers) on every page, one size up (`--ty-k: 1.0625`). Nothing is orange: every orange or brown-orange text, fill and line in the app is now yellow (`dev/src/noorange.css`, generated by `dev/tools/gen_noorange.py`).

The 5 pairings follow the two-font rule: a heading font with personality, a neutral body font, and figures chosen for clarity. Each one also changes capitals, weights and sizes, so they look clearly different from each other and from Current.

| # | Theme | Headings | Body | Numbers | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | Current | Plus Jakarta Sans | Plus Jakarta Sans | Plus Jakarta Sans | Your Home style everywhere; heavy and friendly |
| 01 | Montserrat + Open Sans | Montserrat | Open Sans | Montserrat | UPPERCASE section titles, labels, table headers and pills |
| 02 | Inter + Roboto | Inter | Roboto | Inter | Light and Swiss; regular-weight body, biggest figures (32px) |
| 03 | Playfair Display + Open Sans | Playfair Display | Open Sans | Open Sans | Editorial; largest serif titles (42px) |
| 04 | Merriweather + Roboto | Merriweather | Roboto | Merriweather totals, Roboto tables | Banker's ledger; compact serif titles and totals |
| 05 | Roboto Slab + Open Sans | Roboto Slab | Open Sans | Roboto Slab | Workshop; slab titles, labels, headers and figures |

Rules every theme follows:
- Text is near-black (#0E1114 main, #252A30 secondary); nothing is gray or faded.
- Nothing important is under 14px. Status colours keep their meaning: red is late, short or a loss; yellow is order soon or pending; green is done or healthy.
- The Nifti wordmark stays in Plus Jakarta Sans in every theme.

Files:
- `themes/type/ink.css`: generated from the app; removes gray and faded text and scales every size.
- `themes/type/base.css`: role rules (titles, labels, numbers, tables, pills, tabs, buttons, menu).
- `themes/type/t00-current.css` … `t05-robotoslab.css`: one token file per theme.

The live app embeds ink, base and Current. The lab adds one token file and sets `data-ty`. To switch the live app to another theme, embed its token file and change `data-ty` on `<html>`.

## Stage 6 · Quality check

Each theme was checked on all 22 pages and Edit history at desktop width, and on Home, Orders, Cash count and Stock at phone width. An automated scan of every page found no orange, no gray text and nothing under 14px in any theme. The checks:

- **Premium:** no default-looking components.
- **Typography:** a deliberate pairing, with tabular figures for money.
- **Colour:** every colour has one meaning.
- **Tables and cards:** consistent radii, borders and headers.
- **Buttons:** hover, press, disabled and focus states.
- **Readability:** no text below 14px in the main views, and near-black.
- **Status colours:** the same everywhere.
- **No horizontal scrolling** on phones.

Rejected along the way, per the brief:
- dark-heavy layouts;
- purple-to-blue gradients;
- glass effects;
- pastel "toy" tiles;
- exaggerated rounding.
