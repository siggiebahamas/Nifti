Round 38: chat folders and team board

Chats ×5Team board ×5

Chat categories, stacked top to bottom

Today Team, Customers and Suppliers sit side by side as tabs. Here they run down the side instead, with room for New and Personal. Each one is shown on a computer and on a phone. Color is kept to one small accent, so it never fights the red counts.

Where do personal chats go?Into a Personal folder, always last and grayed. They never show red counts, never show in the team's lists, and their messages stay on your phone (Nifti keeps only the name, so it knows to leave that chat alone). Tap it to see who's in there, and move anyone back to Customers or Suppliers in one tap.

Team board: To do, Doing, Done

Same jobs in every version. The colors mean the same thing everywhere: blue = not started, amber = someone is on it, green = done, and red only for late.
