B11Questions
mga madalas itanong

Questions people usually ask.

Something else? Start free and message us from inside the app.

Do my customers or suppliers need to download an app?

No. They get a link by Viber or SMS, open it on any phone, and tap Confirm. No sign-up.

Can I bring in my old notebook or Excel file?

Yes. Take photos of your notebook pages and Nifti reads them, or upload your Excel or Google Sheets file, or type them in like a spreadsheet. You check every line before it's saved.

Does Nifti hold my money?

No. You get paid directly, by GCash, Maya, bank or cash, like today. Nifti keeps the record and reminds people to pay.

Do I need a computer?

No. Nifti works on any phone with internet. A computer is nice for bigger screens, but not needed.

Who can see my orders and prices?

Only you, the people you add to your team (each sees only their part), and the customer or supplier on that order.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

You go back to Free. Every record stays, and you can download everything any time.
