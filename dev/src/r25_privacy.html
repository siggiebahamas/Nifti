Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Plain words, no fine print. This says what Nifti really does with your information today. It follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

What we keep

  • Your account. Your name, email, business name and type, and the people you add to your team with their roles.
  • Your business records. Orders, prices, stock and stock counts, shop sales, expenses, cash counts, payroll, money owed both ways, credits and returns, your books, team tasks and notes, and the messages you send to customers and suppliers in Nifti.
  • Your customers' details. Names, phone numbers and emails you type in for your own customers. Only add people who are OK with it, because you are the one in charge of their details.
  • Photos you add. Receipts, delivery proof, product photos and notebook pages. They stay with your business until you delete them.
  • Payments you record. Nifti never holds or moves money. We keep what you write down: method, amount, reference number, and a receipt photo if you add one. We never ask for card numbers, PINs or passwords to your bank or GCash.
  • Telegram, if you link it. Your Telegram chat number and the name on your Telegram account, so we can send you alerts. Nifti can only send to that one chat. It cannot read your other chats or contacts.
  • Your online store. A count of visits and orders from your store link, and where the visit came from (for example Facebook or a QR poster). We don't track who the visitors are.
  • Help messages and plan payments. What you write to the Nifti team under "Need help?", any file you send us for setup, and the reference number or receipt photo you send when you subscribe. Only the Nifti team sees these.
  • Team settings. Which pages each role can open, credit slips from your suppliers with their photos, and your weekly post plan, so they work on every phone in your team.
  • Technical logs. Basic errors and which browser you use, to keep the app working.

Who else handles it, and why

  • Supabase stores the database and runs sign-in. Everything travels over encrypted connections.
  • Anthropic, only when you tap to read a notebook page. The photo goes to Anthropic's AI (Claude) so it can read the handwriting, and the lines come back to you. Under Anthropic's business terms, your photos are not used to train their AI.
  • Telegram, only for people who link it. Alerts go through Telegram to your own chat.
  • Phone notification services (from Google, Apple or your browser maker) deliver alerts to phones that turned them on.
  • An email service sends the emails you ask for, like reminders to your customers.
  • The businesses you trade with. A supplier sees the orders you send them. A customer sees the prices and statements you share. Businesses you have not connected with see nothing.

We do not sell your information. We don't share it with anyone else unless you say yes, or the law requires it.

Who inside your business sees what

Each person only sees what their role allows (see Settings, Who sees what). Owners and General Managers see everything. A cashier, for example, doesn't see payroll. The database itself enforces this, not just the screens, so one business can never read another business's records.

How long we keep it

As long as your account is open. When you delete your business account, we remove your records within 30 days. Copies in our backups are removed as the backups roll over. Businesses you traded with keep their own copy of the orders you shared, shown as "Deleted business".

Your rights

  • See and download everything. Settings, Your data, then "Everything in Excel".
  • Fix mistakes. Edit your records any time, or ask us to.
  • Delete. Settings, Your data, then "Delete my business account".
  • Ask or complain. Write to us first. You can also go to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

How we protect it

Role permissions are checked on every action. An activity log records who changed what. Nifti's own admin notes about a business are kept apart where businesses can't read them. Service keys are kept on the server and never sent to your phone. What we don't have yet: an outside security audit or a security certification.

Age

Nifti is for businesses. You need to be at least 18 to open an account.

Changes

When this notice changes in a way that matters, we tell you in the app before it takes effect.
